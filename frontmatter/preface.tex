\addchap{About This Template}

This template gives you a clean starting point for a book, a set of course
notes or a long report on portrait A4 pages, in English with full support
for Spanish. It is deliberately small: a handful of well-known packages, the
default \LaTeX{} font and a single lightweight figure, so it compiles in a few seconds and behaves the same on Overleaf and
on a local \TeX{} installation.

The book you are reading doubles as its own documentation. \Crefrange{chap:getting-started}{chap:citations}
explain each feature and then show it in action, so you can compare the
\LaTeX{} source with the typeset result. \Cref{app:customization}
lists every setting you are likely to change, and \cref{app:checklist}
gives a short checklist for replacing the sample content with your own.

When you are ready to start writing, delete this preface, keep the parts of
the structure you like and make the book yours.
